The "Nothing-Slips" Operational Control Framework

Before marking any rent resolved, moving money, or answering a landlord, verify these 8 operational questions:

1. WHO is involved?

Identify tenant, landlord entity, guarantor, contractor, or utility provider with exact legal name.

2. WHERE does it belong?

Pinpoint property address, specific room (HMO unit), tenancy ID, and client bank account.

3. WHAT happened?

Rent received, partial shortfall, missing payment, duplicate credit, or repair invoice logged.

4. WHEN did it happen?

Exact bank posting date, rent due date, and accounting period cut-off.

5. HOW MUCH is involved?

Exact currency amounts (£), VAT breakdown where applicable, and net balance due.

6. WHY did it happen?

Scheduled monthly rent, deposit installment, holding deposit offset, or unexplained mismatch.

7. DOES IT TIE?

Reconciles between Bank Statement $\leftrightarrow$ COHO Rent Schedule $\leftrightarrow$ AST Contract.

8. WHAT HAPPENS NEXT?

Close account, send arrears reminder, request manager approval, or escalate legal compliance.
