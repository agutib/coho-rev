# Rev's 5-Minute Daily Operating Guide
**COHO OpsHub — Property Operations & Financial Control Center**

Welcome to your daily operating dashboard! This web app is built to save you hours of manual cross-checking between bank statements, COHO rent rolls, and tenant messages.

---

### How to Open the App
1. Navigate to `c:\Users\Bong\Desktop\Automation\COHO-Property-Operations\`.
2. Double-click `start-app.bat` (or open `index.html` directly in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge).
3. Bookmark the tab in your browser for fast daily access!

---

### Your 5-Minute Morning Routine

```
[ Step 1: Download ] ➔ [ Step 2: Drop & Match ] ➔ [ Step 3: Review Exceptions ] ➔ [ Step 4: 1-Click Comms ]
Download Bank CSV      Drop file into OpsHub      Check red & amber rows          Copy polite message to
& COHO Rent Roll       Auto-reconciles instantly  (Missing & Partial arrears)     tenant or WhatsApp
```

#### Step 1: Export Your Morning Records
* Download your latest **Bank Statement CSV** (Lloyds, Barclays, NatWest, Starling, etc.).
* Download or export the active **COHO Rent Roll / Tenancy List**.

#### Step 2: Drag & Drop into the Intake Box
* Drag your bank CSV or COHO file right into the big dropzone at the top of the app.
* *First time or want to practice?* Click **"⚡ Load Demo Data"** in the top right to instantly see 10 rooms and sample bank transactions in action!

#### Step 3: Review the Reconciliation Table
The app automatically matches each bank credit to the correct tenant and room, color-coding the results:
* 🟢 **CLEARED:** Rent received in full on time. No action needed.
* 🟡 **PARTIAL:** Tenant paid less than expected (e.g. £500 instead of £750). The exact shortfall is calculated for you.
* 🔴 **MISSING:** Rent is due but no bank credit has arrived.
* 🟣 **OVERPAID / SUSPENSE:** Extra funds or unidentified bank credits.

#### Step 4: One-Click Communication
* Need to follow up on arrears? Click the **"Remind"** button next to that tenant.
* A polite, professional, and factual email/message is generated instantly with exact dates and amounts.
* Click **"📋 Copy to Clipboard"** and paste it directly into your email, COHO portal, or WhatsApp!

#### Step 5: Check the HMO Compliance Radar
* Click the **"🛡️ HMO Compliance Radar"** tab.
* Look for anything marked **Expired** (Red) or **Due Soon** (Amber).
* This ensures Gas Safety (CP12), EICR, HMO Licences, and 30-day DPS deposit registrations never expire silently.

#### Step 6: Landlord & Manager Reporting
* When Carly, Tom, or a landlord asks for a property update:
* Click **"📋 Landlord Report"** at the top right, type the property name (e.g., `24 Elm Grove`), and copy the executive rent collection summary!
* Click **"📥 Export CSV"** to save a complete reconciled spreadsheet for your records.

---

### The 8-Question "Nothing-Slips" Habit
Whenever a tricky issue arises (e.g. an ambiguous payment or dispute), click the **"Audit"** button on that row. The app will auto-generate your **Nothing-Slips 8-Question Audit** (`Who, Where, What, When, How Much, Why, Does it Tie, What Next`) so you always have bulletproof proof.
