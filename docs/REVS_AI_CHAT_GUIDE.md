# 🤖 Rev''s Guide to Talking with the COHO AI Assistant
**How to get help, teach the AI your property rules, and shape the app**

Welcome, Rev! The AI assistant in the bottom-right corner of your screen isn''t just a basic chatbot. It is trained specifically on **UK HMO Property Management, COHO workflows, and a full 6-agent Finance Suite**. 

Best of all: **it learns directly from you.**

---

## 1. 🧠 How to Teach the AI (Continuous Skill Learning)

Whenever you have an operational rule, tenant habit, or landlord preference that you want the AI to remember forever, use phrases like:
- **"Remember this rule: ..."**
- **"Save this: ..."**
- **"Add to skill: ..."**
- **"Note that tenant/landlord..."**

### Real-Life Examples to Copy & Paste:

| What You Want to Teach | What to Say to the AI |
|---|---|
| **Tenant Payment Alias** | *"Remember: Tenant Dave in Room 3 pays from his mum''s account under the name 'Sarah Smith'."* |
| **Landlord Approval Limit** | *"Remember: Landlord John wants quotes over £200 approved before booking work."* |
| **Contractor Rates & Terms** | *"Remember: Electrician Gary charges £65/hr and requires 24 hours advance notice."* |
| **HMO Council Tax / Utility Rule** | *"Remember: For 14 Oak Street, council tax is included in rent, but tenants split heating overages."* |
| **Tenancy Routine** | *"Remember: Room 4 at Victoria Road is on a 6-month AST renewing on the 1st of October."* |

> 💡 **What happens next:** The AI confirms it saved the rule, stores it in the property operations memory, and automatically pushes it to GitHub. Next time you ask about that tenant, landlord, or contractor, **it will already know!**

## 2. 🏛️ How to Use the 6 Finance Specialists

You have 6 financial experts built right into the chat. You can ask them questions anytime:

### 📒 1. Bookkeeper & Controller
* *"Help me do month-end close for 14 Oak Street."*
* *"How do I track down this £750 unallocated bank credit with no reference?"*
* *"Give me a month-end checklist for rent reconciliation."*

### 💳 2. Accounts Payable (AP) Specialist
* *"Audit this plumber's quote: £350 for replacing a shower cartridge. Is that reasonable?"*
* *"What should I check before paying Gary's plumbing invoice?"*
* *"Our gas bill at Elm Grove jumped from £210 to £480 this month. What should I check?"*

### 📊 3. Financial Analyst
* *"Room 2 has been vacant for 3 weeks at £650/month. Calculate our void loss including council tax."*
* *"What is the gross vs. net yield on a 6-bed HMO generating £4,200/month with £1,100 operating expenses?"*

### 📈 4. FP&A Analyst
* *"Forecast our cash flow for next month based on 9 occupied rooms and scheduled boiler service."*
* *"How should I plan maintenance budget reserves for an older Victorian HMO?"*

### ⚖️ 5. UK Property Tax Strategist
* *"Is replacing all communal carpets in the HMO a tax-deductible repair or a capital improvement?"*
* *"Can we claim capital allowances on furniture and appliances in the shared HMO kitchen?"*

### 💼 6. Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
* *"How large should our sinking fund reserve be for a 10-bedroom portfolio?"*
* *"When is it safe to release the quarterly landlord profit distribution?"*

## 3. 📋 How to Complain or Suggest App Improvements

**You are the boss of this application.** If something annoys you, is confusing, or you wish a button existed: **just tell the AI!**

### Examples of What to Say:
* *"I find it annoying that the table doesn't have an export to PDF button."*
* *"The CSV upload date format was confusing."*
* *"Can you add a button to filter by property name?"*
* *"This font is a bit hard to read on my laptop screen."*

> 💡 **What happens next:** The AI immediately logs your feedback into `docs/USER_FEEDBACK_LOG.md` under `Pending Assessment` and commits it to GitHub so **Arnold and the development team can review it and build it for you!**
