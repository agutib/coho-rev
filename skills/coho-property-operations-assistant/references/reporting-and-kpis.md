# Reporting and KPIs

Use this workflow to report portfolio, property, room, rent, arrears, maintenance, occupancy, and profitability performance.

## Define Before Calculating

For every metric, state:

- business question and decision supported;
- portfolio, entity, property, room, or tenancy scope;
- date range and comparison period;
- numerator, denominator, inclusions, and exclusions;
- source system and as-of time;
- treatment of void, unavailable, newly added, or partially active rooms;
- whether values are cash, accrual, budget, expected, or actual.

Do not compare metrics with different definitions or periods.

## Common Metrics

- **Occupancy rate:** occupied available room-days divided by available room-days, unless the organisation defines it differently.
- **Void days:** available room-days without a paying occupancy under the approved definition.
- **Lost rent:** expected rent attributable to defined void time; label assumptions about asking rent, contracted rent, and proration.
- **Rent collection rate:** rent payments allocated for the period divided by rent due for the period. State whether historic arrears collections are excluded.
- **Arrears:** opening arrears plus period charges less allocated payments and approved credits, using the organisation's convention.
- **Revenue per room:** defined property revenue divided by the relevant room or occupied-room basis.
- **Maintenance cost:** maintenance expenses for the period, shown with one-off and recurring items separated when material.
- **Property operating result:** defined property income less included operating expenses. Do not call this net yield or investment return unless the required capital and cost definitions are supplied.
- **Turnaround time:** elapsed time between the approved move-out-ready and move-in or occupancy milestones.

## Analysis

Reconcile totals before explaining drivers. Separate observed facts from hypotheses. Investigate changes through occupancy or voids, rent rate, payment timing, arrears, maintenance, utilities, supplier pricing, one-off costs, property additions or removals, and data-quality changes.

Use an answer-first summary followed by a metric table:

`Metric | Current | Comparison/Target | Variance | Definition | Primary evidenced driver | Confidence | Action`

Flag incomplete coverage, missing periods, definition changes, small denominators, data corrections, and unreconciled totals.
