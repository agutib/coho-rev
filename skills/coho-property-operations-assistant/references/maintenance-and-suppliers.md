# Maintenance and Suppliers

Use this workflow to administer a maintenance issue from report through financial close-out.

## Maintenance Record

Capture:

- issue ID, report date, reporter, property, room, and affected tenant;
- factual description, photos or documents, access constraints, and known safety impact;
- priority under the organisation's policy;
- assigned owner and supplier;
- quote, approval limit, approver, and approval date;
- appointment, completion evidence, and tenant confirmation when applicable;
- invoice number, amount, VAT evidence, property allocation, and payment status;
- landlord-reporting status and remaining action.

Do not diagnose technical causes or assign legal responsibility without evidence. If the description suggests an immediate risk to people, security, utilities, fire safety, gas, electricity, water, or the habitability of the property, flag it promptly for the responsible emergency process.

## Workflow

1. Confirm that the issue is logged once and linked to the correct property or room.
2. Classify it using the organisation's emergency, urgent, routine, preventive, compliance-related, tenant-damage, or improvement categories.
3. Check authority and spending limits before instructing work.
4. Track quote, approval, access, appointment, and completion.
5. Match the invoice to the work, supplier, approval, and completion evidence.
6. Allocate the expense correctly and prepare owner or internal reporting.
7. Close the issue only when the defined completion evidence exists.

## Supplier Checks

Maintain the supplier's legal or trading name, service type, contacts, coverage, tax or VAT details when evidenced, insurance or qualification documents when required by policy, invoices, payment status, and related work orders.

Flag duplicate invoices, unexplained price changes, invoices without work records, work without approval, missing completion evidence, or payment details changed outside the approved verification process.

Use a tracker with:

`Issue ID | Property/Room | Priority | Supplier | Approval | Appointment | Completion | Invoice | Payment | Owner | Next action | Due date`
