# Reconciliation and Property Bookkeeping

Use this workflow for bank matching, transaction categorisation, COHO-to-Xero checks, property allocations, period cut-off, and owner reporting.

## Transaction Review

For each item, establish:

- bank date, accounting date, amount, currency, and direction;
- payer or payee and bank reference;
- legal entity and bank account;
- property, room, tenancy, landlord, or supplier allocation;
- transaction type and evidenced category;
- invoice, receipt, maintenance record, contract, or other support;
- VAT evidence and treatment, when supplied by an authorised source;
- payment, posting, matching, and approval status;
- whether the item already exists elsewhere.

Do not categorise from the payee name alone when multiple treatments are plausible. A transaction can match the bank while still being allocated to the wrong property, room, tenant, supplier, category, or period.

## Reconciliation Layers

Check only the layers relevant to the task:

1. Bank transaction to supporting evidence.
2. Payment to rent schedule or supplier invoice.
3. Transaction to property, room, tenancy, supplier, and category.
4. COHO total to the relevant bank activity.
5. COHO record to Xero or the nominated accounting system.
6. Property income and expenses to an owner statement or settlement.
7. Opening balance plus movements to closing balance.

Document timing differences separately from errors. For accruals, prepayments, depreciation, VAT, or cut-off adjustments, apply only the organisation's documented accounting policy; otherwise prepare the evidence for accountant review.

## Controls

- Check for duplicates using amount, date, reference, counterparty, and source identifier.
- Confirm that deposits, client funds, rent, fees, refunds, and business income have not been confused.
- Do not offset unrelated differences to force totals to agree.
- Keep proposed correcting entries separate from posted entries.
- Record who approved any adjustment, write-off, refund, or reallocation.

## Output

Provide a reconciliation summary with opening balance, additions, reductions, expected closing balance, recorded closing balance, and difference. Follow it with an exception table:

`Source ID | Date | Amount | Current treatment | Expected treatment | Evidence | Difference reason | Proposed action | Approval required`

When reviewing an owner statement, show gross rent, other income, property expenses, management fees, approved adjustments, prior balance, and proposed net settlement as distinct lines. Do not state that money is payable until ownership, cleared funds, deductions, and approvals are verified.
