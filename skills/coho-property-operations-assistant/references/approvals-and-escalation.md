# Approvals and Escalation

Use this reference whenever the requested action could move money, change contractual or accounting records, affect a tenancy, communicate a legal position, or close a material exception.

## Administrative Assistance

Normally safe to prepare or perform when authorised by the user's role and supported by evidence:

- organise and validate records;
- reconcile an obvious match while preserving source references;
- upload or link a supplied document;
- categorise a routine item under an approved mapping;
- prepare trackers, exception reports, and draft communications;
- follow up on routine missing information;
- document an established SOP.

## Obtain Approval

Prepare the evidence and proposed action, but obtain the designated approval before:

- refunds, write-offs, payments, credits, or financial adjustments;
- rent changes or retrospective schedule edits;
- reallocating an ambiguous payment;
- approving unusual expenditure or exceeding a spending limit;
- changing supplier or beneficiary payment details;
- changing an accounting treatment not covered by policy;
- issuing a commitment, settlement, or owner payment;
- closing an exception without the required evidence.

## Escalate Promptly

- possible fraud or altered payment details;
- client-money ownership, segregation, or reconciliation uncertainty;
- material unexplained financial difference;
- suspected duplicate or unauthorised payment;
- serious or immediate property-safety concern;
- missing or expired potentially material compliance evidence;
- disputed tenant charge, deposit, damage, or legal notice;
- request for legal, tax, VAT, or accounting-policy interpretation outside documented guidance;
- personal-data exposure or suspicious access request.

## Escalation Package

Provide:

`Issue | Risk/impact | Records affected | Amount/deadline | Evidence checked | Immediate containment | Missing information | Decision required | Decision owner | Needed by`

Do not hide uncertainty. When materiality, approval limits, or the responsible owner are unknown, say so and ask the manager to identify the applicable policy or decision maker.
