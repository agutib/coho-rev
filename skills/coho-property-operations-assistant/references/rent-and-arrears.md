# Rent and Arrears

Use this workflow to compare rent expected with payments received and to prepare follow-up work.

## Required Evidence

Gather what is available for the selected period:

- property, room, tenancy, and tenant identifiers;
- tenancy start and end dates;
- rent amount, frequency, due dates, and evidenced rent changes;
- opening tenant balance;
- payment date, amount, payer reference, bank reference, and allocation;
- credits, adjustments, refunds, or write-offs and their approvals;
- the previous follow-up status.

State which items are missing. A bank payment is evidence of cash received, but not necessarily evidence of the correct tenant, tenancy, property, or rent-period allocation.

## Review Sequence

1. Confirm that the tenancy was active for the relevant charge period.
2. Reconstruct the expected rent schedule, including evidenced changes and prorating rules. Do not invent a proration convention.
3. List received payments independently from the rent schedule.
4. Match only when the payer, reference, timing, amount, and tenancy evidence are sufficient.
5. Calculate the closing position using the organisation's sign convention. A common reconciliation is: opening arrears + charges - allocated payments - approved credits = closing arrears.
6. Compare the calculated result with COHO and explain every difference.
7. Classify each exception and prepare the next action.

## Exception Types

- missing or late payment;
- partial payment;
- overpayment or credit balance;
- duplicate payment;
- unidentified payer;
- payment matched to the wrong tenant, tenancy, room, or property;
- incorrect amount or frequency;
- rent change not reflected in the schedule;
- charges continuing after tenancy end;
- room move not reflected;
- deposit or other receipt treated as rent;
- unexplained difference between calculated and displayed balance.

Do not allocate an ambiguous payment merely because the amount is similar to expected rent.

## Output Table

Use columns such as:

`Property | Room | Tenancy/Tenant ID | Due date | Expected | Received | Allocated | Difference | Status | Evidence gap | Next action | Owner | Due date`

Separate confirmed arrears from unverified balances. For follow-up messages, do not accuse the tenant; state the amount expected, amount recorded, dates checked, and the evidence or response requested.
