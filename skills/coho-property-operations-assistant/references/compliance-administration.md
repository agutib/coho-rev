# Compliance Administration

Use this workflow for administrative tracking of certificates, licences, inspections, deposits, insurance, and tenancy documents. It supports monitoring and escalation; it does not determine legal compliance.

## Record Fields

For each requirement, capture:

- organisation or entity, property, room, or tenancy;
- requirement type;
- issuing body or provider;
- document or reference number;
- issue, effective, expiry, renewal, or due date as applicable;
- uploaded-document location and version;
- current administrative status;
- responsible owner;
- next action and internal due date;
- evidence last checked and as-of date.

Possible tracked items may include gas-safety documents, EICR, EPC, HMO licence, legionella assessment, insurance, fire-safety records, inspections, deposit records, right-to-rent records, and tenancy documents. The applicable requirement and deadline must come from current authoritative guidance or the organisation's approved compliance register, not from this list alone.

## Status Rules

Use explicit statuses such as `current`, `due soon`, `expired`, `missing`, `pending verification`, and `not applicable—approved basis recorded`. Do not treat an uploaded file as current without checking its property, scope, dates, and version.

Apply the organisation's reminder windows. If none are provided, propose reminder dates but label them as recommendations rather than compliance deadlines.

## Escalate

Escalate missing, expired, contradictory, or unreadable evidence; an occupied property with a potentially material requirement gap; uncertainty about applicability; and any case where work, a tenancy decision, or a legal notice depends on compliance status.

Use a tracker with:

`Property/Room/Tenancy | Requirement | Reference | Effective date | Expiry/Due date | Status | Evidence link | Owner | Next action | Internal due date`

When current legal interpretation is requested, use authoritative UK or relevant devolved-nation sources and state jurisdiction and source date, or recommend review by the responsible compliance or legal professional.
