# Communications

Prepare concise, factual messages for tenants, landlords, suppliers, accountants, managers, and internal operations staff.

## Message Structure

1. **Situation:** identify the property, room, tenancy, invoice, payment, or issue without exposing unnecessary personal data.
2. **Finding:** state what the available records show, including exact dates and amounts.
3. **Action taken:** explain what has already been checked or prepared.
4. **Request or recommendation:** ask for the missing evidence, confirmation, approval, payment, or decision.
5. **Timing:** give a clear response or follow-up date when appropriate.

Use neutral language for discrepancies. Avoid alleging non-payment, damage, fraud, fault, breach, or legal liability unless established and approved. Do not include sensitive bank, identity, or tenancy data beyond what the recipient needs.

## Common Drafts

- **Rent follow-up:** expected amount and due date, payment amount recorded, records checked, difference, and evidence or response requested.
- **Landlord update:** issue or financial position, evidence, operational or financial effect, action taken, decision required, and deadline.
- **Supplier query:** work or invoice reference, discrepancy, supporting information required, and payment-review status without promising payment.
- **Accountant handoff:** entity and period, transaction or balance, treatment currently shown, evidence gathered, unresolved question, and materiality if known.
- **Internal escalation:** risk, affected records or people, immediate containment already taken, evidence, decision owner, and required response time.

## Internal Note

Alongside an external draft, record:

`Date | Recipient | Subject/Record ID | Evidence used | Message purpose | Response needed by | Next follow-up | Owner`

Do not state that a message was sent unless a tool result or the user confirms delivery.
